Merged Direction: Modern Dashboard + Hybrid Context Panel

This design combines the polished, card-based Modern Dashboard layout with the always-visible queue panel from Hybrid Dense. Both panels are collapsible to reduce clutter when needed.

  • Collapsible left sidebar - Click the arrow button to collapse to icons-only (240px → 64px)
  • Dismissable right panel - Click the × to hide queue/activity; FAB appears to restore
  • Card-based stats and subscriptions - health-first, visual hierarchy
  • Health banner prominent - "calm technology" status at a glance
Try it: Use the controls below or the buttons in the mockup to toggle panel states.
